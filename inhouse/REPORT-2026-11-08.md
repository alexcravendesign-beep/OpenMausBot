### Core-change proposal (awaiting Nick's approval — not started)
- **Why a skill alone cannot enforce F2 & F3**:
  A skill instructs compliant models how to structure `brief.json` and `deliverables.json` and how to call `coordinate_bots`, `attach_file`, and `show_html`. However, a prompt/skill cannot *mechanically enforce* the invariants when a model slips:
  1. A lead model issuing a correction can omit one of the job's earlier participants from `coordinate_bots.bot_ids`.
  2. A lead model can forget `rework: true` when sending a correction to a teammate whose rev 1 turn already settled, causing `coordinate_bots` to refuse the call.
  3. A model can write `"acknowledged": true` in `brief.json` or `"public": true` / `"state": "published"` in `deliverables.json` without a real teammate turn or human approval.
- **Proposed minimal core change (2 files, ~38 lines total, 0 new dependencies)**:
  1. `server/brief-revision-guard.ts` (new, ~30 lines): tracks `{ revision, participants, ackedByGen }` per root job thread and validates `deliverables.json` receipts (enforcing monotonic revision numbers, full participant fan-out on revision bumps, automatic `rework: true` for settled participants when revision > 1, and `public: false` unless a human approval event exists on the thread).
  2. `server/host/question-routing.ts` (+8 lines around `relay()`): calls `briefRevisionGuard` when `coordinate_bots` carries a brief revision or settles a child turn, automatically stamping the verified `briefRevision` and participant acknowledgement state onto the assignment report returned to the lead.
